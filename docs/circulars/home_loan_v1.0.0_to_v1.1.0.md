## Annex: new rule text

**DB-HL-001 Eligible purposes** (Changed; RBI CF para 90, 93, 95)  
A Demo Bank home loan may be used to purchase a new or resale house or flat, to construct a house on a plot the borrower owns, to purchase a plot together with construction (composite loan), to extend, repair or renovate an existing house, or to take over a home loan from another lender. For plot purchase, the borrower must give a declaration to complete construction within 3 years of first disbursement. Finance to extend, repair or renovate a house already financed by Demo Bank must stay within the overall loan ceiling for that house and needs a cost estimate certified by an engineer or architect.

**DB-HL-002 Ineligible properties and uses** (Changed; RBI CF para 92)  
Demo Bank does not finance: property in an unauthorised colony, unless the colony has been regularised and the development and other charges have been paid; property without a sanctioned building plan; or a residential property the applicant intends to use for commercial purposes.

**DB-HL-003 Eligible applicants** (Changed; Bank discretion)  
Only individuals may apply: resident Indian individuals who are salaried, self- employed professionals or self-employed non-professionals. Partnership firms, LLPs, companies, trusts, societies and HUFs cannot be applicants or co-applicants, including on a takeover from another lender; a partner or director may apply only in their individual capacity. NRI and PIO applicants are not covered by this policy version.

**DB-HL-004 Age at entry and at maturity** (Changed; Bank discretion)  
The applicant must be at least 21 years old on the date of sanction. The loan must be fully repaid by age 65 for salaried applicants without pension and by age 70 for self-employed applicants and pension-earning applicants. Co-applicants must be at least 18 years old. A pension-earning applicant is one who already receives a pension or annuity, or who will receive a monthly pension from the employer's scheme after retirement: a defined-benefit pension (government, public sector undertaking or bank), or the National Pension System (NPS), for any NPS subscriber, government or private sector. Proof: pension payment order, or an employer certificate naming the pension scheme, with the PRAN statement for NPS.

**DB-HL-032 LTV for composite loans (plot plus construction)** (New; RBI CF para 111, 90)  
For a composite loan (plot purchase plus construction), the LTV limit in DB-HL-009 is applied to the combined cost: the plot price plus the construction cost estimated by the bank's valuer, using the band for the total loan amount. The plot part of the loan may not exceed the same LTV percentage of the plot price and is disbursed first; the construction part is disbursed in stages linked to construction progress (DB-HL-026). Stamp duty and registration on the plot are not part of the cost (DB-HL-010). Example: plot Rs 20 lakh plus construction estimate Rs 35 lakh gives a combined cost of Rs 55 lakh; a loan in the Rs 30-75 lakh band may be up to 80% of that, Rs 44 lakh, of which at most Rs 16 lakh against the plot.

**DB-HL-017 Prepayment and foreclosure** (Changed; RBI RBC para 352, 353)  
No prepayment or foreclosure charge applies to any floating-rate home loan to an individual, whatever the source of funds and with no lock-in period. This is stated in the sanction letter, the loan agreement and the Key Facts Statement. A charge Demo Bank has waived earlier is never levied later, at prepayment or otherwise.

**DB-HL-018 Penal charges for late payment** (Changed; RBI RBC para 351)  
If an EMI is not paid by its due date, a penal charge of 3% per annum is levied on the overdue EMI amount only, for the days it stays overdue. It is a charge, not an addition to the interest rate, and no interest is charged on it. The amount and reason are disclosed in the loan agreement and Key Facts Statement and stated in every reminder. The penal charge is also published on Demo Bank's website. Each time it is levied, the borrower is told the amount and the reason. No penal charge is levied on unpaid penal charges.

**DB-HL-033 Purchase of one floor of a building** (New; RBI CF para 92)  
Demo Bank finances the purchase of one floor of a residential building (an independent or builder floor) only if: the floor has its own registered title, meaning a sale deed for that floor with a proportionate undivided share in the land; the building's sanctioned plan covers that floor and it was built as per that plan (DB-HL-020); and the floor has independent access. The mortgage covers the floor and its undivided share in the land, and LTV (DB-HL-009) is applied to the value of that floor alone. A floor without separate title, such as an unpartitioned share of a family house, is not financed.

**DB-HL-034 Second house on land that already has a house** (New; RBI CF para 91, 92, 93, 94)  
A borrower who already owns a house may get a loan to build or buy a second house for self-occupation, including a second house on the same land as the existing one. If that land is already mortgaged to Demo Bank, the new loan is sanctioned as additional finance, the mortgage is extended to the new building, and both loans together must stay within the DB-HL-009 LTV limit on the value of the land and both buildings, using the band for the combined loan amount. If the land is mortgaged to another lender, Demo Bank lends only after taking over that existing loan (DB-HL-001), so that both loans are with Demo Bank; it does not share the security through a pari passu or second charge. In every case the sanctioned plan must cover the second building (DB-HL-020), and repayment capacity (DB-HL-011) counts both EMIs.

**DB-HL-025 Key Facts Statement** (Changed; RBI RBC para 348)  
Before sanction, every applicant receives a Key Facts Statement with a unique proposal number, the APR and the amortisation schedule, in a language they understand. Bank staff explain its contents to the borrower and obtain the borrower's acknowledgement that they have understood it. It is valid for at least 3 working days, and if the borrower accepts within that period Demo Bank is bound by its terms. It follows RBI's standard KFS format and is attached to the loan agreement as a summary box. The KFS includes an APR computation that covers all charges, and lists charges payable to Demo Bank separately from charges payable to third parties through the bank (such as insurance, legal, valuation or stamp duty); where a charge cannot be known before sanction, the KFS shows its upper limit. Third-party charges are recovered at actual cost, with receipts and supporting documents given to the borrower. No charge missing from the KFS may be levied without the borrower's explicit consent.

**DB-HL-028 Return of property documents after closure** (Changed; RBI RBC para 354, 355, 358, 359)  
Original property documents are returned and all charges removed within 15 working days of full repayment, including filing the satisfaction of charge with every registry where it was registered (such as CERSAI), at the branch or another office of the borrower's choice. If the documents are not returned within 30 days, Demo Bank tells the borrower the reason for the delay, and if the delay is attributable to Demo Bank it pays the borrower Rs 5,000 for each day of delay. If documents are lost or damaged, Demo Bank helps the borrower obtain duplicate or certified copies at its own cost within a further 30 days, after which the daily compensation applies.
